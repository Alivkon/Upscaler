# Theme tags made by looking at every picture

An earlier pass (research/2026-09-20-collection-candidates.md) assigned themes
from catalogue text alone. Charlie looked at its twelve "ancient ruins" and
said one of them actually shows ruins. This pass redoes the tagging by opening
the image.

## Method, in three lines

1. Live works listed the way `.themesheet.mjs` does it: catalogue entry not
   `hidden: true`, present in `images/manifest/`, resolved manifest to phone
   crop, plain scan preferred, the copy nearest 480px wide. 158 works.
2. Every one of those 158 images was opened and looked at, one at a time, full
   tile, never as a montage cell. Titles were read only after looking.
3. Judged on what is visible in the phone crop, because the crop is what the
   visitor gets. Five works were also checked against the full plate and it is
   said so in the line.

## Known limits

- One pair of eyes, one pass, no second opinion; "clear" and "partial" are a
  judgement, not a measurement.
- The phone crop is the unit. A work whose ruin or autumn tree lives outside
  the 9:16 cut is tagged on what the cut shows, and the line says so.
- "stormy-sea" and "dark-floral" were not tagged: demand was measured at zero.
- Nothing in `collections.js` was touched.
